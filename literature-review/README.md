## How to use a paper file
Each file follows `_templates/paper-template.md`: verified APA citation, problem, solution, results, limitations, relevance to the router, where to cite it in the report, and a ready Table 2.3 row. The header says whether the source was read in full and whether the paper is peer reviewed or a preprint.

## Conventions
- A paper is documented once, in its home topic; other topics link to it.
- Competitor and vendor material is grey literature (not peer reviewed) and was fetched on 2026-10-31. Re-check before submission.
- Preprints are marked as such. Several 2026 papers may have a newer version or venue by submission time.
- Where a file says a number comes from arXiv v1 rather than the camera-ready, check it before quoting.

## Report requirements met (template Chapter 2)
At least 25 works in depth (90), at least 40 references (90 plus grey sources), APA 7 with DOIs where they exist, 2020 or later. The 70%-in-last-five-years rule is met for every paper (all are 2020 or later). Peer-reviewed share varies by topic; see each overview.
