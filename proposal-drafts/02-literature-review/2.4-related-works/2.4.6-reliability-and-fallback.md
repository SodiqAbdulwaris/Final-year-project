### 2.4.6 Reliability and fallback

SpotServe handles preemption and reports a 2.4–9.1-fold reduction in 99th-percentile latency and 54% cost savings in its tested setup (Miao et al., 2024). DéjàVu instead moves or replicates key–value state and reports up to 2-fold throughput and a separate 1.8-fold swapping gain (Strati et al., 2024). Both assume access to engine state, so neither proves that a gateway can resume an interrupted hosted response. ServerlessLLM focuses on checkpoint locality and startup (Fu et al., 2024), a different problem from choosing another model after an API error.

Huang et al. (2022) analyse incidents in which retry amplification can sustain overload, complementing engine recovery studies without testing an LLM gateway. Together these findings justify bounded retries and remaining-budget checks, but the project's recovery success and cost require injected-failure experiments.
