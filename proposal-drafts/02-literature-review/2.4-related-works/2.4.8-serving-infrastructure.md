### 2.4.8 Serving infrastructure

MuxServe multiplexes multiple models and reports up to 1.8-fold throughput or 2.9 times as many requests within 99% service-level attainment (Duan et al., 2024). SGLang instead optimises structured language-model execution and prefix reuse, reporting up to 6.4-fold throughput in its evaluated workloads in the cited preprint (Zheng et al., 2024). Their workloads and baselines differ, and neither number measures this project's gateway overhead. AlpaServe places models across a distributed system (Z. Li et al., 2023), whereas S-LoRA batches adapters sharing a base model (Sheng et al., 2024). Both address deployment efficiency, not the selection of independent hosted and local models for a request. These boundaries support holding the serving backend constant when evaluating the selector.
