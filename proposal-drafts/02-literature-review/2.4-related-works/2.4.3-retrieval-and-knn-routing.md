### 2.4.3 Retrieval and k-nearest-neighbour routing

Varangot-Reille et al. (2026) estimate model performance using similar scored requests across five routing benchmarks and test history sparsity down to 1%; simple k-nearest-neighbour averaging is competitive with their attention-based method. Results remain dependent on labels and neighbour coverage. Zhang et al. (2026) instead use agent histories, reporting 58.7% and 43.4% cost reductions on ScienceWorld and HLE respectively against GPT-5 in the preprint. Their trajectory setting is not a direct test of ordinary gateway requests. A retrieval-only baseline and classifier–retrieval ablation should therefore test whether the added component helps in this project's setting.
