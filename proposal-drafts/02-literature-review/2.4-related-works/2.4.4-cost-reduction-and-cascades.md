### 2.4.4 Cost reduction and cascades

Dekoninck et al. (2025) formulate routing and cascading under a cost constraint, reporting improvements up to 8% on RouterBench and 14% on SWE-Bench in their evaluated settings; these depend on estimation and optimisation assumptions. BEST-Route also connects model choice with sampling computation (Ding et al., 2025), while Gupta et al. (2024) study uncertainty-based escalation and Yue et al. (2024) use reasoning-path agreement. Their additional evidence requires generation or verification, so a fair comparison must count those calls.

LLMLingua reports up to 20-fold compression with limited performance loss on evaluated tasks (H. Jiang et al., 2023), while LongLLMLingua conditions compression on the question (H. Jiang et al., 2024). Both alter the input, unlike selecting a model for the unchanged request. These results support including prompt length in cost estimates but not silently deleting user content.
