### 2.4.7 Benchmarks and evaluation

RouterBench provides over 405,000 stored inference outcomes (Hu et al., 2024); these are not necessarily distinct requests and its archival status is unconfirmed. LLMRouterBench covers over 400,000 instances, 21 datasets, 33 models and 10 routing baselines, including latency-aware analysis (H. Li et al., 2026). Neither historical outputs nor a latency model substitutes for live network and failure measurements. RouterEval also shows why the candidate pool should be specified when interpreting routing results (Z. Huang et al., 2025).

Chatbot Arena's pairwise preferences and HELM's scenario-based multi-metric evaluation capture different aspects of quality (Chiang et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2023). MT-Bench's model judges introduce position, verbosity and self-preference risks (Zheng et al., 2023). EmbedLLM learns model representations from outcome data (Zhuang et al., 2025) and, like retrieval, depends on coverage of those data. Matched held-out requests and separate quality, cost, latency and calibration measures are more informative than one aggregate score.
