## 2.7 Research Gap and Justification

This project investigates whether combining lightweight classification and embedding-derived suitability features with constrained cost–quality–latency selection improves on simpler routing baselines. Operational fallback is evaluated separately for resilience.

The contribution will be the formal decision policy, integration and empirical evaluation. The existence of learned routers, cascades, serving schedulers and commercial gateways will rule out treating their mere combination as proof of first-of-its-kind status. The study will instead compare the hybrid against its components under shared constraints, and will report whether extra feature extraction is justified by the observed trade-off.

**Table 2.4: Research-gap-to-objective mapping**

| Gap identified | Evidence (studies) | Objective that addresses it |
|---|---|---|
| The appropriate request contract and hard constraints must be specified for this project's gateway setting. | Quality-aware routing and constraint-oriented allocation provide related formulations (Ding et al., 2024; Šakota et al., 2024). | O1 |
| The incremental value of classification plus retrieval for this pool has not been established. | Preference routing, contrastive matching and past-performance retrieval offer alternatives (Ong et al., 2025; S. Chen et al., 2024; Varangot-Reille et al., 2026). | O2 |
| The hybrid's cost–quality–latency trade-off relative to simpler methods requires controlled measurement. | Unified benchmark comparisons motivate strong baselines; RouteBalance is adjacent joint-assignment preprint work (H. Li et al., 2026; Da & Kalyvianaki, 2026). | O3 |
| Engine-state recovery does not establish the behaviour of constrained gateway fallback. | SpotServe and DéjàVu operate on serving state (Miao et al., 2024; Strati et al., 2024). | O4 |
| Stored-outcome comparisons must be supplemented with this gateway's live overhead, accounting and failure tests. | RouterBench supports scored replay; LLMRouterBench includes latency-aware analysis; these will not replace measurements of the proposed deployment (Hu et al., 2024; H. Li et al., 2026). | O5 |

These will be project-specific questions and evidence boundaries, not assertions that the literature has never studied constraints, calibration, latency or reliability. Live GPU/queue telemetry, Redis/Prometheus, session hysteresis, managed context and graph memory will remain unresolved earlier proposals. Neither mandatory experiments nor claims of novelty will be built around them without a later recorded scope decision.
