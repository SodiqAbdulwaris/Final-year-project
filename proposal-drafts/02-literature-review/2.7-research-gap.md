## 2.7 Research Gap and Justification

The literature already contains prompt-aware selection, model-specific prediction, cost-aware routing and load-aware serving, and existing products already provide gateway functions. The gap pursued here is narrower. It is not yet established, in the setting to be tested, whether combining task classification with embedding-derived suitability justifies its overhead when hard constraints, hosted and local endpoints and bounded fallback are evaluated together against simpler baselines. Table 2.4 maps the gaps to the objectives of Chapter One. It does not assert that no existing system has any one of these features.

**Table 2.4: Research-gap-to-objective mapping**

| Gap identified | Evidence (studies) | Objective that addresses it |
|---|---|---|
| Uncertain added value of combining task classification with embedding-based per-model suitability | Ding et al. (2024); Ong et al. (2025); S. Chen et al. (2024); Shirkavand et al. (2025); Zhuang et al. (2025) | Objectives 3 and 4 |
| Quality, cost and latency are hard to compare fairly across alternatives, and stored-outcome benchmarks omit live conditions | Dekoninck et al. (2025); Hu et al. (2024); Li et al. (2026); Agrawal et al. (2024) | Objectives 4 and 6 |
| Hard constraints and bounded recovery are not evaluated together in one gateway | Da & Kalyvianaki (2026); Aderaldo & Mendonça (2026); Miao et al. (2024); Romero et al. (2021) | Objectives 1, 2, 5 and 6 |

These are project-specific questions and evidence boundaries. They are not claims that the literature has never studied constraints, calibration, latency or reliability. Live GPU and queue telemetry, session memory and graph-based retrieval are not part of the committed core.
