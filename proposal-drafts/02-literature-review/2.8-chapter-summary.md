## 2.8 Chapter Summary

The review will frame the project around prediction, constrained selection and execution reliability. Pre-generation routers, cascades and fusion systems will be distinguished; difficulty and confidence will be separated from model-specific suitability; retrieval will be tested against simpler predictors; and serving-engine results will not be transferred directly to an API gateway.

The selected works will support a controlled comparison with strong baselines, component ablations and explicit quality/cost/latency measures. Dated vendor documentation will establish that routing and recovery features already exist in practice. The proposed contribution will therefore remain an inspectable decision policy, integration and empirical evaluation. Chapter 3 will translate those questions into requirements, design and a pre-specified protocol.
