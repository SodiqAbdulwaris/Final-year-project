## 2.8 Chapter Summary

The reviewed evidence indicates that pre-generation routing can reduce expensive-model use, that retrieval-based estimates must be compared with simpler predictors, and that engine-level scheduling or recovery results do not directly establish gateway performance. The remaining gap is the lack of a controlled evaluation in this project's gateway setting of a combined task-classification and retrieval predictor with constrained cost–quality–latency selection and separately tested operational fallback. Chapter Three will specify the requirements, design, baselines and evaluation procedures needed to test that gap.
