## 2.5 Review of Existing Systems

The comparison will use official vendor documentation accessed on 31 October 2026. These sources will be grey literature, not peer-reviewed evidence of comparative performance. “Not established” will mean that the checked material did not establish a feature, not that the product cannot provide it.

**Table 2.2: Feature comparison from vendor documentation**

| System | Per-prompt selection | Execution fallback | Local/self-hosted context | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OpenRouter | Auto Router documented. | Model/provider fallback documented in routing index. | Self-hosted deployment not established by Auto Router page. | Current Auto Router describes market/task signals; older “provider selection only” wording is obsolete. |
| LiteLLM | Auto Router documented separately from core load balancer. | Cooldowns, retries, timeouts and fallbacks documented. | Deployment-based routing supported; exact local engine configuration will require testing. | Core load balancing will not be mistaken for a learned suitability predictor. |
| Portkey | Conditional policies documented; learned suitability not established by fallback page. | Prioritised provider/model fallback and retry controls documented. | Custom hosts/self-hosting referenced, exact deployment contract untested. | Feature documentation, not measured success under this project's faults. |
| TrueFoundry | Official current guide describes complexity-based routing. | Cross-provider retries/fallbacks described. | Official guide describes self-hosted and commercial backends. | Earlier review's blanket “no per-prompt choice” and “local not documented” are outdated. |
| Not Diamond | Pretrained and custom intelligent routing documented. | Not established by overview page checked. | Self-hosting not established by overview page checked. | Do not copy marketed savings as independent results. |
| Proposed router | Task classification and retrieval-informed constrained selection are planned. | Bounded, constraint-preserving operational fallback is planned. | Hosted and local adapters are planned, subject to implementation and hardware. | Proposed features are not implemented or experimentally established. |

The evidence URLs are [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/routers/auto-router), [LiteLLM](https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/routing), [Portkey](https://portkey.ai/docs/product/ai-gateway/fallbacks), [TrueFoundry](https://www.truefoundry.com/blog/ai-agent-portability), and [Not Diamond](https://docs.notdiamond.ai/docs/what-is-not-diamond). These are vendor sources accessed 31 October 2026; publication dates were not established and should be recorded as n.d. in the bibliography unless verified.

The project will not claim novelty from providing one endpoint, routing per prompt or offering fallback. Its contribution will be the specified constrained policy and empirical comparison, including component ablations and separately evaluated operational recovery. Product features and policies will be rechecked before submission because documentation and offerings change.
