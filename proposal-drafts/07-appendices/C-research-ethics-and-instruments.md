# Appendix C: Research Ethics and Instruments

## Basic-ethics record

No formal ethical approval was sought or required for the proposed public-data/document-based study, under the owner's stated study position. Basic research ethics will include honest reporting, no intentional personal-data collection, source attribution, anonymised records and informed consent if people are involved. This will not be presented as an institutional exemption certificate.

## Participant scope

The default plan will involve no recruited participants. Informal internal design walkthroughs will not be reported as a representative user study. If a participant activity is later approved within scope, this appendix will add a plain-language consent statement and questionnaire before recruitment.

## Conditional instrument register

| Instrument | Condition for creation | Required contents | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consent form | People will provide research responses. | Purpose, voluntary participation, withdrawal, data use, anonymity and contact route. | Not drafted: no participant study selected. |
| Questionnaire/task guide | A defined participant question is added. | Neutral prompts, no identifiers, minimal response burden and analysis plan. | Not drafted: no participant study selected. |

No signed forms, participant identities or invented responses will be included.
