## 3.11 Chapter Summary

This chapter will guide development of the hybrid router through traceable requirements, separate prediction/selection/reliability responsibilities and a reproducible evaluation protocol. Hard filters and conservative accounting will apply before every dispatch, including fallback. The constrained policy will prefer the cheapest feasible candidate, while component ablations will test whether classification and embeddings justify their cost.

The data protocol will prevent evaluated outcomes from entering training or retrieval. Quality, cost and latency comparisons will be separated from reliability tests, and provisional targets will be frozen before test inspection. Chapter 4 will later contain the implementation and measured evidence; it will remain empty of results at proposal stage.
