### 3.6.7 Security and Privacy

The gateway will authenticate callers, separate administrator permissions and validate payload sizes, context/output caps and authorised endpoints. Deployment URLs will be administrator-controlled. Server-side request forgery protections will validate schemes, destinations and redirects while permitting only explicitly authorised local endpoints. Provider credentials will remain outside client responses, model prompts and routine logs.

Input text will be treated as untrusted data. Instructions embedded in a request will not change model permissions, budget accounting, secret access or retry policy. Per-request and shared spending reservations will be rechecked transactionally before dispatch. Tool calls or other side effects will require an explicit safe-retry contract; transport failure will not establish that an earlier action did not occur.

The project will minimise retained data, use anonymous request identifiers and prefer aggregate observations. Raw personal or confidential data will not be intentionally collected or sent to hosted models. Logs and exports will be redacted; access, retention and deletion will follow a documented policy. The retention period and secrets-management mechanism will remain TODO.

The Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) will be considered in the design. The official Nigeria Data Protection Commission materials identify principles of lawful and transparent processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, limited retention and security (official regulatory material, accessed 31 October 2026: [NDPA source](https://www.ndpc.gov.ng/ndp-act-2023/)). These are regulatory requirements, not peer-reviewed paper evidence or a claim of completed compliance. If personal-data processing is later introduced, the team will document its lawful basis, notices, access/deletion process, recipient restrictions and applicable cross-border arrangements before collection. This will not be inferred from a provider merely offering an API.

Security evaluation will include unauthenticated calls, privilege violations, forbidden endpoints, oversized inputs, secret/redaction checks and fallback attempts that would violate privacy or budget policy. Threats and unresolved controls will be reported with their test evidence. No formal ethics approval will be represented as a substitute for privacy or security obligations.
