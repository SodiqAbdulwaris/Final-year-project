## 3.11 Risk Assessment

Table 3.11 lists the main risks with their likelihood (L) and impact (I), rated high (H), medium (M) or low (L), and a mitigation for each.

**Table 3.11: Risk assessment**

| Risk | L | I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| No aligned scored outputs for the chosen models | M | H | Check coverage in Phase 1; pick models from the aligned subset; record the limitation. |
| Local model does not fit laptop memory or runs too slowly | M | M | Profile both laptops in Sprint 2; use a smaller quantised model and treat it as a separate configuration. |
| Hosted API spending exceeds the budget | M | M | Hard spending cap enforced in the gateway; replay for most experiments. |
| Dataset licence blocks use or sharing | M | H | Clear licences in Appendix G before use; share only configurations and aggregate results. |
| Provider changes an API, price or availability during the project | H | M | Pin versions and record dates; keep adapters isolated. |
| Laptop thermal throttling distorts timing | H | M | Fixed power mode, repeated runs, recorded temperatures; report variability. |
| Hybrid shows no gain over simpler baselines | M | L | Report it as a valid finding; ablations and baselines are already planned. |
| Scope creep (extra dashboards, memory features) | M | M | Freeze requirements after Sprint 2; extras stay optional. |
| Team workload and exam periods delay sprints | H | M | Two-week sprints with slack before deadlines; named owners and backups; supervisor review. |
