## 3.15 Chapter Summary

This chapter chose Design Science Research for the study and Scrum with two-week sprints for the building work, and it set out how requirements will be gathered from documents, benchmark data and scenarios. It stated the requirements, a preliminary design with the routing algorithm, the data and the tools. Every objective has a method, a metric, a target and a baseline, and the data protocol keeps evaluated outcomes out of training and retrieval. The chapter closed with the ethical position, the risks, a sprint plan, an estimated budget in naira and the expected outcomes. Chapter Four will report the implementation and the measured evidence.
