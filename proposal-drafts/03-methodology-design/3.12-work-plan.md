## 3.12 Work Plan

Work follows two-week Scrum sprints from November 2026 to May 2027, with the report due in June 2027. Table 3.12 gives each sprint, its owners and its deliverable. Owners follow the roles proposed in the team’s task list; the roles will be confirmed with the supervisor, and the sixth member will be given tasks at the first sprint planning meeting. Evaluation and report writing overlap with implementation and do not wait for it. The Gantt chart and the record of supervisor meetings will be kept in Appendix F.

**Table 3.12: Sprint plan**

| Sprint | Period | Owners | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Requirements and protocol | Nov–Dec 2026 | Solomon, Wahida | Requirements, dataset coverage check, licence register, experiment protocol |
| 2. Gateway core | Dec 2026–Jan 2027 | Waris, Maryam | API, validation, registry, database; laptop profiling |
| 3. Adapters and baselines | Jan–Feb 2027 | Waris, Maryam, Kabiru | Hosted and local adapters; fixed, cheapest and rule-based policies; logging |
| 4. Features | Feb 2027 | Solomon | Task classification, embeddings and k-NN index |
| 5. Predictor and selector | Mar 2027 | Solomon, Waris | Suitability predictor, calibration, constrained selector, ablation configurations |
| 6. Reliability | Mar–Apr 2027 | Waris, Maryam | Health tracker, circuit breaker, bounded fallback, accounting |
| 7. Testing and deployment | Apr 2027 | Maryam, Kabiru, Wahida | Fault-injection tests, deployed gateway, operator view |
| 8. Evaluation | Apr–May 2027 | Solomon, Maryam | Replay evaluation and capped live pilot |
| 9. Analysis and report | May 2027 | All; Wahida leads documentation | Result tables, final report draft |
