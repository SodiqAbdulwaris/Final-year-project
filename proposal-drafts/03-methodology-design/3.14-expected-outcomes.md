## 3.14 Expected Outcomes

The project is expected to deliver the following, each linked to an objective of Section 1.3.

- A requirements specification, preliminary design and traceability matrix (Objective 1).
- A working, documented gateway with adapters, eligibility checks, constrained selection and logging (Objective 2).
- A suitability predictor with ablation results on held-out scored requests (Objective 3).
- A comparison of the constrained selector with simpler baselines on quality, cost and latency (Objective 4).
- A reliability mechanism with fault-injection results (Objective 5).
- A deployed gateway, a test and evaluation package, deployment instructions and the final report (Objective 6).

A conference or workshop paper may follow if the results are clear. The project does not promise a positive result: a simpler router that matches the hybrid is an acceptable and reportable finding.
