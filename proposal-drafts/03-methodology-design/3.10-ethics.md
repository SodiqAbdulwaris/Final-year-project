## 3.10 Ethical Considerations

No formal ethical approval was sought or required for the document-based, public-data study described in this proposal, and basic research-ethics practice was followed in preparing it. This statement will record the owner's specified study position; it will not represent an approval certificate or independently obtained institutional exemption.

The planned experiments will use permitted public benchmark material, synthetic requests where appropriate and redacted operational records. The team will avoid personal data, preserve anonymity, respect source and dataset rights and report findings honestly. Outputs will not be fabricated, selectively omitted or presented as evidence of human review that did not occur.

Human involvement will be minimal or absent. If people are later involved, informed consent, voluntary participation and anonymised responses will precede collection. No identifying raw prompts, names or contact details will be part of the experimental dataset. A change to the participant or data scope will require the team to check the applicable institutional process before proceeding.

Generative-AI use will be declared fully, including tool/version where known, task, chapter/section and verification method. Unknown model versions will remain placeholders. Team members will read the key cited papers themselves and correct summaries where necessary. Appendix E will distinguish automated source checks from personal full-text reading.
