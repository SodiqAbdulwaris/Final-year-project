# CHAPTER THREE: METHODOLOGY, SYSTEM ANALYSIS AND DESIGN

This chapter explains how the project will be carried out and how its success will be judged. It states the research design and the development model, how requirements will be gathered, the existing and proposed systems, the requirements specification and a preliminary design that includes the routing algorithm. It then sets out the data, tools and evaluation plan, with a target for every objective, followed by the ethical considerations, risks, work plan, budget and expected outcomes. Because this is a proposal, the chapter is written in the future tense and the designs are preliminary. A reader with similar skills should be able to repeat the project from this chapter alone.
