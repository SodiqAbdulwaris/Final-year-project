## 1.4 Research Questions

The project will answer the following research questions, one for each objective.

RQ1. Which request, capability, budget, privacy and reliability requirements must a routing decision satisfy for a deployment to be eligible?

RQ2. How can a gateway be structured so that validation, eligibility rules, routing policies and logging can be configured and checked against its requirements?

RQ3. To what extent do task classification and embedding-derived features contribute, separately and together, to model-specific suitability estimates on held-out requests?

RQ4. To what extent does constrained hybrid selection change cost and latency while preserving quality, compared with fixed-model, cheapest-eligible, rule-based and lightweight learned baselines?

RQ5. How do health tracking, circuit breakers and bounded fallback affect request completion and constraint compliance under injected execution failures?

RQ6. To what extent does the deployed gateway meet its correctness, overhead, observability and reproducibility requirements under documented workloads?

The quality comparison will use a margin and scoring protocol fixed before the held-out results are examined. Cost and latency changes will be measured, not assumed. Chapter Three sets out the hypotheses, metrics and sample selection.
