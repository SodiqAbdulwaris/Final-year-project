## 1.4 Research Questions

| ID | Research question | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | What request constraints and reliability requirements will define an eligible routing action? | O1 |
| RQ2 | To what extent will classification and embedding-derived features contribute to model-specific suitability and calibration on held-out requests? | O2 |
| RQ3 | To what extent will constrained hybrid selection reduce cost and latency while preserving quality relative to the best single model and simpler routing baselines? | O3 |
| RQ4 | How will health tracking, circuit breakers and bounded fallback affect completion and constraint satisfaction under injected execution failures? | O4 |
| RQ5 | What correctness, overhead and reproducibility properties will the integrated gateway exhibit under normal operation and controlled concurrency? | O5 |

The confirmatory quality comparison will use a non-inferiority margin chosen and justified before the test set is examined. Cost and latency comparisons will assess reductions rather than assume them. Section 3.9 and Appendix F will specify the hypotheses, units of analysis, intervals and handling of inconclusive results.
