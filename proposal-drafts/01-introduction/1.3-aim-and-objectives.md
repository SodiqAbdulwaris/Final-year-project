## 1.3 Aim and Objectives

The aim will be to design, implement and evaluate a hybrid prompt-aware LLM routing algorithm with constrained cost–quality–latency selection and automatic operational fallback through a common gateway.

| ID | Objective | Deliverable and measurement | Completion milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | Specify the request, capability, budget, deadline and reliability requirements and trace them to acceptance tests. | Requirements tables, policy definitions and requirements-to-test matrix. | Requirements freeze, [PLACEHOLDER: requirements-freeze date] |
| O2 | Develop a lightweight model-suitability predictor using task classification and embedding-derived evidence, and compare its components on held-out scored requests. | Predictor and ablation definitions; quality and calibration measures. | Predictor freeze, [PLACEHOLDER: predictor-freeze date] |
| O3 | Develop the constrained selector and compare its quality, cost and latency with fixed-model, cheapest-eligible, rule-based and lightweight learned baselines. | Reproducible comparisons; non-inferiority within a justified margin and cost/latency reductions [PROVISIONAL: revise after pilot]. | Evaluation freeze, [PLACEHOLDER: evaluation-freeze date] |
| O4 | Integrate hosted and local adapters with health tracking, circuit breakers and bounded operational fallback. | Constraint-preserving recovery tests, fallback success and attempt accounting. | Integration milestone, [PLACEHOLDER: integration date] |
| O5 | Evaluate software correctness, gateway overhead and reproducibility under normal operation, concurrency and injected failures. | Traceable test evidence, p95 timing, violation counts and an experiment report with uncertainty. | Final evaluation, [PLACEHOLDER: final-evaluation date] |

Each objective will yield a specified artefact and a measurable comparison. The milestone dates will make the schedule time-bound once the team supplies its calendar. No objective will require a positive result: a simpler method matching the hybrid will remain a reportable outcome. The margin and thresholds will be fixed after a pilot and before test outcomes are examined.
