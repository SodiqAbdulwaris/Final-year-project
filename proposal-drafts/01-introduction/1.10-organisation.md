## 1.10 Organisation of the Report

Chapter 1 will introduce the problem, aim, questions, scope and limitations. Chapter 2 will review the search method, conceptual foundations, related routing and serving research, existing gateways and the evaluation gap. Chapter 3 will specify requirements, system design, data preparation, security and the experiment protocol.

Chapter 4 will later describe implementation, testing and measured results. It will remain a skeleton at proposal stage. Chapter 5 will later summarise the findings and make evidence-based recommendations without introducing new results. The references will include the works actually cited. Appendices will hold search provenance, licence checks, ethics materials, test traceability, AI-use records, the experiment protocol and diagram sources.
