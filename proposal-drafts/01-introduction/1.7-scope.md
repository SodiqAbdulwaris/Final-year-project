## 1.7 Scope

The core will include deterministic capability, context-window, budget and availability filters; lightweight task and capability classification; model-specific suitability prediction; constrained quality, cost and latency selection; hosted/local execution adapters; circuit breakers; bounded operational fallback; and monitoring. The evaluated pool will contain three to four benchmark-covered models spanning a cheap or small option, a mid-tier option and a frontier option. Final names will remain [PLACEHOLDER: model names] until coverage and aligned scored outputs are established.

Embedding similarity will be included as a recommended component whose value will be tested through ablation. Quality-based cascading will be an optional experiment, distinct from operational fallback. Reward-guided learning will remain future work. Reinforcement learning (RL), partially observable Markov decision processes (POMDPs) and response fusion will be excluded initially. The project will not train or fine-tune the underlying language models.

Live GPU and queue telemetry, Redis/Prometheus, session hysteresis, router-managed context and graph memory will be recorded as **unresolved earlier proposals**. They will not be silently made core requirements or treated as permanently rejected. No core objective or result claim will depend on them unless a later explicit scope decision is recorded.

The study will cover offline replay, a capped live pilot and controlled local failure tests. It will not establish production-scale availability, universal quality guarantees, distributed GPU scheduling or seamless continuation of a partially delivered stream on another model.
