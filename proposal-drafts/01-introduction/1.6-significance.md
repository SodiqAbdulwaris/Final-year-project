## 1.6 Significance

**Practical significance.** For developers, the project will provide a common interface to hosted and local models, with controls over eligibility, budgets and recovery. The gateway will record why a candidate was excluded or chosen and how recovery proceeded, which supports debugging without keeping raw sensitive prompts in routine logs. For Nigerian teams paying in dollars or handling personal data, an explicit policy layer makes cost and data-location choices visible. These benefits depend on implementation and testing, and the project does not assume that every application will save money.

**Academic significance.** The project will connect requirements, design decisions, tests and experiments for an AI-dependent service, and will evaluate budget accounting, failure handling and privacy restrictions as software behaviours. It will provide a controlled comparison of the hybrid approach against its own simpler components and will report the cost of feature extraction and selection next to any inference savings. A competitive simple baseline or an inconclusive comparison will still show which design is appropriate. The findings will apply to the models, workloads and deployments tested.
