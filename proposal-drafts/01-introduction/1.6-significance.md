## 1.6 Significance

For developers, the project will provide an inspectable decision policy and a common interface for hosted and local models. The proposed system will expose why a candidate was excluded or selected, which constraints applied and how recovery proceeded. These explanations will support debugging without requiring raw sensitive prompts in routine logs.

For software engineering, the work will connect requirements, design decisions, tests and experiments. Budget accounting, failure handling and privacy restrictions will be evaluated as software behaviours rather than inferred from model-quality scores. The traceability matrix will show how each requirement will be checked.

For routing research, the main value will be a controlled comparison of the hybrid against its own simpler components. The study will report the cost of feature extraction and selection alongside any inference savings. An inconclusive comparison or a competitive simple baseline will still inform the choice of an appropriate implementation.

For the project team, reuse of scored outputs and a bounded local/live evaluation will make the study feasible under limited API funding. The findings will be qualified by the selected model pool and machines; the project will not claim to represent all providers or national infrastructure.
