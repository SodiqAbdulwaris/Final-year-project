## 1.8 Limitations

The following limitations are outside the team’s control. Each is followed by how it will be handled.

- Model access and budget. API quotas, a minimal spending budget and two laptops with RTX 4070 graphics cards (a Lenovo Legion 7i and an ASUS ROG Zephyrus G16) will restrict the models and the pilot size, and laptop thermal and power limits may reduce sustained performance. The model pool and measured hardware specifications will be recorded, and conclusions will be limited to those configurations.
- Provider variability. Model versions, prices, rate limits and availability may change during the project. Versions, dates and pricing assumptions will be recorded.
- Offline evaluation. Stored outputs cannot reproduce current network delays, throttling or outages. Live requests and injected failures will supplement the offline results, and the two will be reported separately.
- Benchmark coverage and scoring. A model listed in a benchmark may not have aligned scored outputs with the other candidates, and automatic scores may miss aspects of usefulness. Only comparable records will be used, and datasets, scoring procedures and limits of generalisation will be reported.
- Hybrid strategy. Classification and embedding evidence may add overhead without improving on simpler decisions. Ablations and baselines will test whether the added complexity is justified.
- Limited human evaluation. No formal ethics approval is sought and human involvement will be minimal, so claims about usability and user acceptance will be limited.
- Evaluation scale. Time may restrict concurrency tests and sample sizes. Test conditions will be reported and claims will stay within the evidence observed.
