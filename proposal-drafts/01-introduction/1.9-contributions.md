## 1.9 Expected Contributions

The project will aim to contribute a formal routing policy that separates hard eligibility, suitability estimation and constrained selection; an integrated gateway with auditable operational recovery; and empirical comparisons that isolate the value of classification, embeddings and policy constraints.

The evaluation package will include baseline and ablation definitions, a leakage-aware data protocol, failure-injection scenarios, requirement-to-test traceability and reports of quality, cost, latency and calibration. Sharing will be limited to materials whose redistribution rights have been checked. Configurations and aggregate analyses will be preferred over redistribution of uncertain third-party datasets.

These will be planned contributions, not completed achievements. The project will make no first-of-its-kind or fundamentally-new-machine-learning-algorithm claim. Its empirical contribution will remain useful if the hybrid does not outperform a simpler method.
