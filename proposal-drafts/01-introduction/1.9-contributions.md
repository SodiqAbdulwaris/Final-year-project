## 1.9 Expected Contributions

The project will aim to deliver the following contributions. They are proposed, not completed results, and any claim of improvement will rest on measurements reported with their uncertainty.

- A requirements specification and a traceability matrix linking gateway features, policies and quality attributes to acceptance tests (Objective 1).
- A documented, working and deployed request-routing gateway with configurable adapters, eligibility checks, constrained selection and logging (Objectives 2 and 6).
- A hybrid model-suitability predictor with reproducible ablations that show the contribution, if any, of classification and embedding-derived evidence (Objective 3).
- A comparison of the constrained selector with simpler routing baselines on quality, cost and latency (Objective 4).
- A health-tracking and bounded-fallback mechanism with failure-injection results (Objective 5).
- A monitoring view of routing decisions, estimated or recorded costs, latency, provider status and fallback events, limited by what the integrated providers expose (Objectives 2 and 6).
- A testing and evaluation package and deployment instructions sufficient to reproduce the tested setup within stated resource and access limits (Objective 6).

The project makes no claim to be the first of its kind or to introduce a fundamentally new algorithm. Its empirical contribution remains useful if the hybrid does not outperform a simpler method.
