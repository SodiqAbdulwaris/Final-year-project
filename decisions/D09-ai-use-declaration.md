## The question
This literature review and the drafts were produced with Claude Code (a lead session plus sub-sessions and fact-checking agents). How will you declare and verify it?

## What I found
- The template requires stating tools, tasks, chapters and how output was verified; undeclared AI-generated text presented as your own is misconduct.
- Every paper file records its source and verification date, but the files are AI-written summaries. The Scopus template says never cite a paper you have not read.
- An independent fact-check of 16 sampled papers found no fabricated numbers but did find about 20 wording and figure errors that were fixed; that suggests error rates are not zero (see L01, L07).

## Recommendation
Declare it in the table (tool: Claude Code; tasks: literature search, paper summaries, first drafts of chapter skeletons, fact-checking; chapters 1-3). Split the 89+ papers among members and have each read the full text of the papers they cite, correcting the files as they go. Keep a short log of what was changed.

Your own reading is what makes the citations safe; the review is a starting map.
