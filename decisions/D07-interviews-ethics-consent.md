## The question
Who will you interview (>=5) and test (>=8 for SUS), how will consent work, and does the department require ethics approval?

## What I found
- Guide requires at least five developer interviews and preferably eight usability participants, with consent and no identifying details stored.
- Template (3.10) requires consent description, anonymisation and whether ethical approval was obtained; the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 applies where personal data is processed.
- The usability study with ~8 participants is formative, not statistical proof (guide).

## Options
| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Fellow students/developers at Nile | Fast; risk of narrow sample |
| Mix students + junior developers from outside | Better validity; more effort |
| Interviews anonymous, no recordings | Simplest consent route |

## Recommendation
Recruit 5-8 developers (students plus a few outside developers), anonymous notes only, written consent form in Appendix C, and ask the supervisor in writing whether formal ethics approval is required. Use a 10-item SUS and report the mean with an interval.

## Consequences and things to watch
Do not collect names, emails or raw prompts. Interview questions should be open so they do not confirm your design.
